## Contributing

All contributions to this project — code, documentation, anything in the repo — must be made under a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) so the project owner can offer the dual-licensing model. Two CLAs exist:

- **[CLA.md](./CLA.md)** — individual contributors (contributing on your own time, with your own copyright).
- **[CLA-CORPORATE.md](./CLA-CORPORATE.md)** — employees contributing on behalf of a company that holds the copyright in their work.

By opening a pull request, you confirm you have read the relevant CLA and agree to its terms. Future CLA-bot automation is tracked as a follow-up.

## Why dual-license?

The AGPL is the right default — it keeps modifications open. But it's also incompatible with most companies' source-code policies, which means companies that want to build on top of this couldn't legally do so. The commercial alternative gives them a path while preserving the AGPL guarantee for everyone else.

The CLA is what makes the dual model possible. Without it, contributions would be governed by the AGPL the contributor chose, and the project owner couldn't grant commercial sub-licenses on top of those contributions. With it, all contributions assign or license enough rights upstream that the owner can offer the dual-license to commercial customers.
