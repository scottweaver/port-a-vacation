# Corporate Contributor License Agreement — Port A 2026

Thank you for your organization's interest in contributing to Port A 2026 (the "Project"), maintained by Scott T Weaver (the "Project Owner"). This Agreement clarifies the intellectual-property rights granted with each Contribution made by an authorized employee, contractor, or agent of the Corporation (defined below).

This Agreement is **for corporations and other legal entities** whose employees or agents contribute to the Project as part of their employment or engagement. Individual contributors contributing on their own time (with no employer claim to the IP) should use [`CLA.md`](./CLA.md) instead.

---

## 1. Definitions

- **"Corporation"** means the legal entity making this Agreement with the Project Owner, identified in the executed signature block at the bottom of this document.

- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any modifications or additions to an existing work, that is intentionally submitted to the Project by an authorized employee, contractor, or agent of the Corporation, for inclusion in, or documentation of, the Project. "Submitted" includes pull requests, attached code on issues, emails to the Project Owner, or any other form of electronic, verbal, or written communication — but excluding communication conspicuously marked or otherwise designated in writing as "Not a Contribution."

- **"You"** means the Corporation and its Authorized Contributors collectively.

- **"Authorized Contributors"** means employees, contractors, and agents of the Corporation who the Corporation has designated as permitted to make Contributions to the Project on the Corporation's behalf. The Corporation is responsible for maintaining the list of Authorized Contributors and notifying the Project Owner of any changes.

The Corporation will maintain a list of Authorized Contributors and provide it to the Project Owner upon request. Contributions submitted by an individual not on the Authorized Contributors list, on behalf of the Corporation, are not covered by this Agreement and shall be governed by the [Individual CLA](./CLA.md) by default.

This Agreement requires a written signature from an authorized representative of the Corporation, in addition to a list of Authorized Contributors. To execute, email <scott.t.weaver@gmail.com> with:
