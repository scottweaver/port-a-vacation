# v3.16 — Drive plan with realistic stops

The drive card was showing pure driving time (3h 40m) with cumulative offsets like `+0:50`. That's fine in theory but doesn't reflect how the trip actually goes — you stop at Buc-ee's, you stop at H-E-B, you stop for gas. Recalculated with real stop durations and switched to clock times.

## What's new

- **~30 min stop at Buc-ee's, ~30 min at H-E-B, ~15 min gas/bathroom break** baked into the timeline.
- **Times are now 12-hour clock times** (11:00 AM, 11:50 AM, 2:55 PM…) instead of offsets from departure — easier to read at a glance.
- **Recommended departure: 11:00 AM CDT** for a 3:55 PM arrival (just before 4 PM check-in).
- Drive card header now shows both numbers: `~5 hrs with stops · ~3.5 hrs driving`.

## Caveat

Memorial Day Monday could push the Port Aransas ferry past the usual 20-min peak-season wait, which would eat the 5-min cushion before check-in. If you want a 15-min buffer instead, leave Austin at 10:45 AM.

## Under the hood

- `DRIVE_STOPS` in `src/lib/trip-data.ts` — `time` field is now a wall-clock string (`"11:00 AM"`) instead of an offset (`"0:00"`); notes mention stop durations.
- New constants: `DRIVE_DRIVING_HOURS` (pure drive time, 3.5), `DRIVE_DEPART_TIME`, `DRIVE_ARRIVAL_TARGET`.
- `DriveCard.tsx` subtitle split into two lines (totals + recommended departure); `+{stop.time}` rendered as just `{stop.time}` since these are no longer offsets.

No schema change.
