# v3.2 — Live filter

Find any item without scrolling.

## What's new

- **Live filter** — a search box under the header on the Trip tab. Type a word (say "sunscreen") and the dashboard collapses down to only the checklist sections that match — every other card hides out of the way. Categories that were collapsed open up automatically so you can see the matches. Clear the filter (the X in the box, or just hit Esc) to bring everything back.
- **Version visible on mobile** — the small `v3.2 (abc1234)` chip under "Port A 2026 🏖️" now shows on phones too, not just on desktop. Handy when reporting a bug from your phone.

## Under the hood

- Filter does a case-insensitive substring match against item labels. No debounce — 74 items is trivial.
- Hidden items stay hidden under the filter — searching doesn't bypass your hide list.
- `CollapsibleCard` got a new `forceOpen` prop so the filter can override the per-user collapsed state without clobbering it. When you clear the filter, your collapsed sections snap back to how you left them.
