# v3.6 — Confetti! 🎉

The countdown deserves a celebration when it hits zero.

## What's new

- **Trip-start confetti.** The moment the countdown ticks past 0:0:0:0 on Monday May 25 at 8 AM CDT, the app fires off a Gulf-palette confetti burst (rose, coral, gold, pink, ocean blue) across the viewport. The header switches to "Trip in progress!" right after.
- **You won't miss it.** If you open the app for the first time AFTER the trip has started (e.g., you weren't watching at 8 AM, or you're on a different device), you still get the celebration on your first visit. Each device gets its own one-time burst — so your phone gets it once, your laptop gets it once.
- **Reloading after celebrating** is quiet. The flag is per-device and persists across page loads, so the confetti doesn't replay every time you refresh.

## Under the hood

- Added `canvas-confetti` (tiny — ~10KB gzipped). Three quick staggered bursts (~½ second total) for a wider visual spread.
- `useEffect` in `CountdownCard` watches `start.isPast`. Single localStorage flag (`port-a:trip-start-celebrated`) gates the celebration — same effect handles both "watching at the transition" and "loading after the fact" cases.
- localStorage failure (private mode, quota exceeded) silently degrades to "confetti on every reload" — annoying maybe, but not broken.
- No schema changes, no prod migration. To reset the flag for testing: `localStorage.removeItem('port-a:trip-start-celebrated')` in the dev console.
