# v3.5.3 — Toolbar no longer shifts

Tiny TopBar polish.

## What's fixed

- **The Online pill (and everything else in the toolbar) stops jumping around** every time the pending-writes badge flashes. The amber sync badge that appears for ~½ second when you tap +/- on a quantity used to push every right-side pill sideways. Now it floats to the LEFT of the Online pill outside the flex flow, so it can appear and disappear without nudging anything.
