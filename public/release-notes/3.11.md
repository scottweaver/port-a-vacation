# v3.11 — Shopping list 🛍️ + clearer icons

A separate shopping list mode (like Pack mode, but explicit opt-in), plus a contrast pass on the action icons next to every checklist item — they were too pale to see clearly, especially on mobile.

## What's new

- **🛍️ Shopping bag icon** on every checklist row. Tap to add the item to your family's shopping list; tap again to remove. The icon fills with amber when the item is on the list.
- **🛒 Shop button** in the toolbar (next to Pack) opens a dedicated shopping screen. Two sections, just like Pack:
  - **Need to buy** at the top — items you've added but haven't bought yet.
  - **Got it** below, collapsed by default — tap the circle to mark something bought (line-through, moves into the collapsed section); tap again to move it back. Each row also has an X to remove the item from the list entirely.
- Toolbar shows a small amber count badge on the Shop button whenever your list has items.
- Family-private: each family has their own list (same privacy model as Pack mode).
- **Clearer action icons** on every checklist row — the chat bubble, shopping bag, hide-eye, and remove-X were rendered at `text-slate-300` which was nearly invisible on the cream card background. Bumped to `slate-500` so they're actually visible without hovering. Same fix on the empty/unchecked Circle icon used in Pack + Shop views.

## Under the hood

- Migration `0010_shopping_list.sql` adds the `shopping_list` table. Composite PK (item_id, family_id) and family-private RLS mirroring `packing_status`. New `purchased boolean` column for the Need/Got toggle (Pack didn't need this since unpacking = delete; Shopping keeps the row around when "Got it" so the buyer can un-bought without losing their place).
- New `useShoppingList` hook follows the standard cache → realtime → queue pattern. Three queue ops: `upsert` (add), `updatePurchased` (toggle), `delete` (remove from list). Family-scoped realtime channel filter so other families' lists don't churn your state.
- `ShoppingView` is a clone of `PackView`'s structure with a few differences: no auto-derivation from `contributions` (membership is explicit); rows have an X for explicit removal (Pack just lets you uncheck back into the top section); `myQuantity` is shown as a `×N` hint when the row also has a family quantity on the planning side, but is purely informational.
- Contrast pass: every action button on ChecklistRow that was `text-slate-300` is now `text-slate-500` (still subordinate to the main item text but readable). Also removed the `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100 sm:opacity-100` dance on the hide + delete buttons — they're always visible now since the icons are properly contrasted.
- No new tests (the hook follows an established pattern that the existing 85 tests already exercise the queue logic for).
